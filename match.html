Before a match

Plan, then execute Detail

  1. Plan standing up. Read the table, choose the shot.
  2. Lock in three things: aim, contact point, stroke and power.
  3. Get down and execute. No re-deciding.
  4. Still thinking? Stand up and go again.

Routine, every shot

Even on easy balls.

Follow first

If follow gets me there, play follow.

The 30° rule

Rolling, quarter to three-quarter ball: the cue ball leaves at about 30°.

Accurate break

On a 7-foot table, a square hit beats raw power.

Breathe and trust

If the line feels wrong, stand up and reset.

Mindset Detail

  • Run the program. No forcing changes in a match.
  • Take your time: look around the table and breathe before stepping in.
  • Adjusting once down? That's a trust problem. Stand up.

The table scan Detail

  1. What's on? What can I pot?
  2. After the break: take the more run-out-able group.
  3. Can I run out? Plan every ball to the black.
  4. If not, find the problem balls.
  5. Plan the visit: run out, pot-pot-safety, solve a problem, or safety only.
  6. Can this visit improve the table for later?

Before every shot Detail

  • Margin for error: if it goes wrong, where am I?
  • Come into the line of the next shot, not across it.
  • Scratch? Trace follow shots to the corner pocket.
  • The black: near it? Avoid it.
  • The other ball: does it still have a pocket?

The break Detail

  • Rack tight, every ball touching. Magic rack.
  • Cue ball just behind the line, one finger left of centre.
  • Align on the centre of the head ball. Stand up, rehearse the flick.
  • Bridge up, one tip below centre. If the cue ball draws back, that's too low.
  • Square and accurate. Loose wrist, flick for power.

Safety Detail

  • Attacking or containing? Go for ball in hand, or just make it awkward.
  • Trouble spot for the cue ball, and can I get it there?
  • Use a problem ball to get there?
  • What if I miss it? My blind spot.
